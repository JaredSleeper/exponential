A New York community

Participating in accelerating change, together.

Exponential is a selective community for people who are building, researching, and thoughtfully applying AI. We gather in small rooms — salons, breakfasts, dinners — to think out loud, compare notes, and form real relationships around the most consequential technology of our time.

How we gather

Small rooms, long conversations.

Salons

Evening conversations around a single question or a member's work-in-progress — hosted, discursive, unrecorded.

Breakfasts

Small morning tables for people in adjacent corners of the field — researchers next to founders next to writers.

Dinners

A handful of members around one table. The point is the people, not the programming.

What we value

Curiosity, contribution, and each other.

Membership is selective and small by design. We look for people who are genuinely engaged with AI — building it, studying it, or applying it with care — and who show up ready to give as much as they take. Gatherings are the center of gravity; the relationships are the point.
